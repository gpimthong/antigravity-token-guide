How AI Tokens Work & Cost

Everything you need to know about Google Antigravity in 3 fun concepts — no boring jargon!

🧱

1. Tokens are Word LEGOs

AI doesn't read full words or sentences like you do. It chops every sentence and piece of code into tiny bite-sized LEGO blocks called Tokens!

🎒

2. The Robot's Backpack

Google Antigravity is a coding robot. Every time it reads a file, fixes code, or runs tests, it carries everything in its memory backpack.

🎟️

3. How It Costs Money

Reading and writing tokens takes robot energy. With a monthly subscription, you get a daily pass! Pay-as-you-go costs fractions of a single penny.

🌟 3 Golden Rules to Save Tokens & Keep It Fast

  • 🎯
    Point directly to the toy: Use @game.js instead of saying "look at all my files". This saves the robot from carrying heavy files it doesn't need.
  • 🧹
    Empty the backpack when done: Hit New Conversation or type /compact when you finish a task so the old stuff doesn't pile up!
  • ⚡
    Use Gemini Flash for everyday tasks: It's lightning-fast and super cheap for homework, small scripts, and everyday bug fixes!
